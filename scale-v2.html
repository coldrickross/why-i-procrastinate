Why a V2?

The first scale used little plus/minus buttons. This one is more physical: every reason is a weight you can grow by clicking on it, or shrink with a right-click. The beam tilts in real time so you feel the decision shifting as you change what's on it.

It's the same idea as before — behaviour is a scale — just with a more direct way to play with it.
