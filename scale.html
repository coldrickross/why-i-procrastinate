How the scale works

Every time you decide whether to do something, your brain is running a quiet little comparison. It piles up every reason for acting on one side, every reason against on the other, and goes with whichever side is heavier.

If you're not taking action, the scale just isn't tilted toward action yet. That's it. It's not a character flaw — it's arithmetic. Procrastination is just the side marked "against" having a bit more weight on it right now.

So the question stops being "why am I so lazy?" and becomes something much more useful: what's on each side of my scale, and how do I change what's there? Add weight to "for". Take weight off "against". That's the whole game.
