How the scale works

Every time you decide whether to do something, your brain is running a quiet little comparison. It piles up every potential reward on one side, every possible cost on the other, and goes with whichever side is heavier.

If you're not taking action, the scale just isn't tilted toward action yet. That's it. It's not a character flaw — it's arithmetic. Procrastination is just the costs side carrying a bit more weight right now.

So the question stops being "why am I so lazy?" and becomes something much more useful: what's on each side of my scale, and how do I change what's there? Add weight to rewards. Take weight off costs. That's the whole game.

Three quick examples
