If this sounds like you

You already know the cost of not starting. It's why you're here.

The site you just used names the feeling. Coaching takes you the rest of the way — through it, not around it. Most clients come to me stuck on one of these:

  • Frozen on the thing that matters most. The big project, the hard email, the conversation you keep rehearsing. Small tasks get done; the important one doesn't.
  • Starting and not finishing. Eleven notebooks. Four half-built websites. Courses at 18% complete. Momentum keeps leaking out.
  • Overwhelm that looks like laziness. The task isn't big. It's that everything feels big, so the body opts out and the inbox wins.
  • Fear dressed up as "I'll do it tomorrow." Fear of judgement, of being seen, of it not being perfect — so you protect it by not doing it.
  • Self-criticism loops. You miss a day, call yourself lazy, feel worse, do less. You know the loop. You can't think your way out of it.
  • Unclear next step. Not a motivation problem — a clarity problem. You can't start because you don't actually know what starting is.
  • Perfectionism. "Not ready yet" is running your calendar. Ready never arrives on its own.
  • No accountability. You can hold everyone else to their word. You can't seem to hold yourself to yours.
  • Avoidance of one specific thing. Taxes. A health appointment. A resignation. A draft. It's cost you months already.
  • Energy flat-lining. It's not that you don't care. It's that nothing feels like it counts anymore.

If two or more of those made you wince — keep reading. This was built for you.
